WhatsApp Chat Viewer

Read, search and analyse an exported chat. Everything stays on your device: nothing is uploaded.

How do I export a chat?

In WhatsApp open the chat, tap the name at the top, then Export chat. Choose Without media for a small .txt file or Include media for a .zip with photos. Save it to your phone and pick it here.

The dates in this file could be day-first or month-first. Pick the right one above if dates look wrong.
